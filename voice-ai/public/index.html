FAQ

Questions business owners ask

Will callers know they're talking to an AI?

VoiceDesk sounds natural, but it's honest. If someone asks, it says it is the business's AI assistant, and it can pass the call to a person at any time.

Can I keep my existing phone number?

Yes. Turn on call forwarding from your current number (for example when busy, unanswered, or after hours) to your VoiceDesk number.

What if it doesn't know an answer?

It only answers from the information you give it. If it doesn't know, it says so and takes a message, which appears in your Leads page.

Is my customers' data safe?

Call records are private to your business account. You can export them anytime, and erase any customer's data on request in one click.

How do I know it's good enough before customers call?

Run the Test Lab. It role-plays difficult callers and grades every conversation, so you see a Readiness Score and exact fixes before going live.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. Setup is a form in your dashboard, and you can test everything from your browser before connecting a number.
